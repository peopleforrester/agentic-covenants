# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be straightforward and be correct. Disagree about the technical substance as sharply as the evidence supports. Do not make it about the person.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any other space this project occupies, including when someone is representing the project at a conference or in a talk.

## What is expected

- **Argue from evidence.** This project's entire value is being right about what actually enforces a constraint. A correction backed by a primary source is welcome from anyone, regardless of how it lands on the maintainer's prior work. Being wrong in public and fixing it is the normal operating mode here.
- **Assume competence.** Contributors come from platform engineering, security, compliance, and federal program offices. Someone unfamiliar with your corner of that is not unqualified, they are unfamiliar with your corner.
- **Keep criticism aimed at the artifact.** "This policy does not deny what the README says it denies, here is the manifest that gets through" is the ideal contribution. "Whoever wrote this does not understand admission control" is the same information with the useful part removed.
- **Accept a decline gracefully.** [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](./CONTRIBUTING.md) lists what gets declined and why. A declined contribution is a scope judgment, not a verdict on the contributor.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation, or sustained personal hostility.
- Demeaning comments about someone's identity, background, employer, nationality, or clearance status.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Deliberately submitting artifacts that are subtly wrong. Given that people copy these templates into production systems, this is treated as the most serious violation on this list.

## Reporting

Report a concern to the maintainer directly at **michael@performantpro.com**, or through GitHub's private reporting on this repository. Reports are handled confidentially. You will get a response, and you will be told what was done.

The maintainer is a participant here, not an exception to it. If the concern is about the maintainer's own conduct and you would rather not raise it with him, say so in the report and it will be routed to a neutral third party.

## Enforcement

Responses scale with severity and history: a private correction, a public one, temporary restriction from interaction, or a permanent ban. Deliberately harmful contributions and harassment can skip straight to the last one.
